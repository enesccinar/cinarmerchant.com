# Cinar Merchant Services — Brand Guide

## Positioning
Cinar Merchant Services is an independent, technology-forward merchant-services brand. The desired impression is modern fintech with enterprise trust: credible for merchants, banks, payment providers, and partners without appearing like a generic ISO/reseller.

## Public name
Use **Cinar Merchant Services** in brand and marketing. Use **Cinar Merchant Services LLC** only where a legal entity name is appropriate.

## Selected logo direction
**Concept A — Connected Network** is the recommended primary direction. The geometric core represents the merchant/payment platform while the two connected nodes suggest payment flow, connectivity, and an extensible network. It avoids literal credit cards, dollar signs, and shopping carts.

Alternative concepts are included in `/public/brand`:
- `logo-concept-a.svg` — recommended; connected payment network
- `logo-concept-b.svg` — transaction flow / movement
- `logo-concept-c.svg` — compact C monogram
- `mark.svg` — square app/social icon based on Concept A

## Color palette
- Midnight Navy — `#081A33` — primary trust/finance color
- Technology Blue — `#2563EB` — primary interactive/accent color
- Cloud White — `#F8FAFC` — main surface/background
- Emerald — `#10B981` — sparing status/success accent
- Slate — `#657084` — secondary copy

## Typography
The implementation uses the system/UI stack with **Inter** first. For production, Inter can be self-hosted or loaded through Next.js font optimization. Avoid overly rounded or playful display fonts.

## Voice
- Clear and confident
- Modern but not trendy
- Business-first
- Avoid exaggerated claims such as “lowest rates,” “instant approval,” or claims of compliance/certification that have not been verified
- Do not publish provider names unless contract/brand requirements support doing so

## Core message
**Payments infrastructure built for growing businesses.**

Supporting statement:
> Modern payment solutions that help businesses accept payments, manage transactions, and scale with confidence.

## Legal / compliance note
The included Privacy Policy and Terms of Use are starter copy intended to make the site structurally complete. Have qualified counsel review them before production use, especially before collecting merchant applications, KYC/underwriting data, or enabling analytics/advertising.
